# Contributor Privacy Notice

Version 1.0

This notice explains how MASSA LABS processes personal data when individuals and
corporations contribute to Gossip, enter into a Contributor License Agreement
(**"CLA"**), or are identified as Designated Contributors under a CCLA.

## 1. Controller

The data controller is **MASSA LABS**, a French _société par actions simplifiée_
(SAS), with its registered office at 226 Boulevard Voltaire, 75011 Paris,
France, registered with the Paris Trade and Companies Register under number
881 320 592 (SIREN 881 320 592).

Questions and requests concerning personal data may be sent to
`info@massa.net`.

## 2. Data covered by this notice

Depending on the contribution and signing process, MASSA LABS may process:

- Identity data, including a signer's full legal name, country of residence,
  GitHub username, and immutable GitHub user ID.
- Contact data, including an email address and, for a corporation, its business
  contact details and registered office.
- Agreement data, including the ICLA or CCLA type, exact agreement version or
  immutable repository revision, acceptance statement, timestamp, signing event,
  and related pull request.
- Contribution data, including commits, authorship and co-authorship metadata,
  issue and pull-request activity, review comments, and repository history.
- Corporate authority data, including a corporation's legal identity,
  registration number, representative's name and title, evidence of authority,
  and Designated Contributor history.
- Technical and audit data supplied by GitHub or the selected signing process,
  such as account identifiers, event identifiers, and security logs.

MASSA LABS does not require an individual ICLA signer to provide a residential
street address through the routine electronic signing process.

## 3. Sources of data

MASSA LABS obtains this data from:

- You when You sign an agreement, communicate with maintainers, or contribute.
- A corporation that identifies its representative or Designated Contributors.
- GitHub and public repository history.
- A signing, hosting, or e-signature provider identified in the signing process.
- MASSA LABS records used to verify corporate authority or contribution rights.

If a corporation provides information about a Designated Contributor or
representative, MASSA LABS will provide or make this notice directly available
to that individual no later than the first communication with the individual,
the first disclosure of the data, or one month after receipt, whichever occurs
first, unless an exception under GDPR Article 14 applies. The corporation must
also ensure that it is entitled to provide the information to MASSA LABS.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

MASSA LABS processes CLA and contribution data for the following purposes:

| Purpose                                                                                                                      | Legal basis                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Taking steps to enter into, recording, and administering an ICLA with an individual signer                                   | Performance of a contract or steps requested before entering into one, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)                                                                                          |
| Processing a corporate representative's or Designated Contributor's identity, authority, and schedule information for a CCLA | MASSA LABS's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f)                                                                                                                              |
| Verifying whether a Contribution may be accepted and whether employer rights are covered                                     | Contract administration under Article 6(1)(b) where the data subject is the individual contracting party, and MASSA LABS's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) in other cases |
| Documenting authorship, software provenance, license grants, and agreement versions                                          | MASSA LABS's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f)                                                                                                                              |
| Preventing fraud, preserving audit records, enforcing agreements, and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims    | MASSA LABS's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f)                                                                                                                              |
| Complying with binding legal, regulatory, or court requirements                                                              | Compliance with a legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c), where applicable                                                                                                           |

MASSA LABS's legitimate interests are maintaining reliable software provenance,
confirming that it can lawfully use and distribute Contributions, operating a
secure contribution process, preventing false signatures, and preserving
evidence needed to enforce or defend legal rights. MASSA LABS has balanced those
interests against contributors' rights by limiting routine data collection,
keeping CLA records private, and retaining public GitHub data only as necessary
for these purposes.

MASSA LABS does not rely on consent as the primary legal basis for administering
the CLA. Withdrawing data-protection consent, where consent applies to a separate
optional activity, does not revoke rights already granted under a CLA.
It also does not affect the lawfulness of consent-based processing carried out
before consent was withdrawn.

## 5. Required and optional information

The identity, authority, agreement-version, and acceptance fields identified as
required in the applicable CLA or signing process are contractual requirements.
MASSA LABS may be unable to accept or merge a Contribution if the information
needed to verify the applicable rights is not provided.

Fields expressly marked optional may be omitted without preventing signature,
although MASSA LABS may request additional evidence if reasonably necessary to
resolve an authorship or authority question.

## 6. Recipients and confidentiality

CLA signature records, completed corporate schedules, and authority evidence are
private. Access is limited to authorized MASSA LABS personnel and service
providers that need the information for the purposes described above.

MASSA LABS may also disclose relevant data to:

- GitHub and the provider used to host or administer the signing workflow.
- Professional legal, security, audit, or technical advisers subject to
  appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- A buyer or successor conducting appropriate due diligence or assuming the
  Project and its CLA obligations.
- Courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, or other recipients when disclosure
  is legally required or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal
  claims.

GitHub contribution activity, including usernames, commits, issues, and pull
requests, is generally public under GitHub's own terms and privacy notice. This
notice does not make already-public repository activity private.

MASSA LABS does not sell CLA personal data.

## 7. Storage, providers, and international transfers

MASSA LABS's designated private repository for CLA records is the private
`massalabs/cla` GitHub repository. GitHub may process data in the United States
and other countries in which GitHub or its subprocessors operate, including
countries for which the European Commission has not adopted an adequacy
decision.

For restricted transfers, GitHub states that it relies, as applicable, on the
EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses adopted by Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914. Details and
copies of the safeguards are available in GitHub's
[Data Protection Agreement](https://github.com/customer-terms/github-data-protection-agreement),
[General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
and [subprocessor list](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-subprocessors).
Questions about obtaining a copy of applicable safeguards may also be sent to
`info@massa.net`.

The final signing implementation has not yet been selected. MASSA LABS will
identify any additional signing provider, storage location, subprocessors,
third-country transfers, and applicable safeguards in this notice or through the
signing process before using that provider to collect personal data.

## 8. Retention

MASSA LABS retains CLA and associated audit records for as long as reasonably
necessary to document rights in Contributions, including while a Contribution is
used, distributed, or licensed, and for applicable limitation periods afterward.
Records may be kept longer where necessary for an actual or reasonably
anticipated legal claim, binding legal obligation, or legal hold.

When records are no longer needed for those purposes, MASSA LABS will delete or
anonymize them. Removal of a Designated Contributor does not require deletion of
the historical authorization needed to document Contributions submitted while
that authorization was effective.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, You may request:

- Access to Your personal data and information about its processing.
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Erasure of data that MASSA LABS no longer has a lawful basis to retain.
- Restriction of processing.
- Objection to processing based on legitimate interests.
- Portability of data where the right applies.

MASSA LABS may retain data needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims
or to comply with a binding legal obligation, including evidence of an
irrevocable copyright or patent license. Exercising a privacy right does not
withdraw or revoke rights already granted under a CLA.

MASSA LABS may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the identity
and authority of a person making a request. Requests may be sent to
`info@massa.net`.

You may lodge a complaint with the **Commission nationale de l'informatique et
des libertés (CNIL)** at <https://www.cnil.fr/> or with another competent data
protection authority.

## 10. Automated checks

MASSA LABS may use an automated repository check to determine whether the
required CLA record exists for a pull request. A missing record may prevent an
automated merge, but the check does not make a legal or similarly significant
decision about an individual without the possibility of human review. Questions
or correction requests may be sent to `info@massa.net`.

## 11. Security

MASSA LABS uses access controls, private storage, audit history, and other
technical and organizational measures intended to protect CLA records. No
storage or transmission method is completely secure. Suspected unauthorized
access to CLA records should be reported to `info@massa.net`.

## 12. Changes to this notice

MASSA LABS may update this notice when the signing process, legal requirements,
or data practices change. Material changes will receive a new version and will
not alter the terms of a CLA already accepted. The version displayed when a
signature is collected will be retained with, or be traceable from, the signing
record.
